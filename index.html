About

I'm a technical lead who thinks in systems. Since 2019 I've built secure, scalable platforms across fintech, ML infrastructure, and confidential computing, with teams in Africa and Europe.

Most of my work is in Rust and Python: taking products from first prototype to production, designing security-focused architectures, and leading the teams that ship them. At Mithril Security I worked on BlindAI and BastionLab — tools for deploying models and collaborating on data without exposing it. My research in parallel and distributed systems was published at ACM SOSP 2021.

What I care about is knowing where the trust boundary actually is, from the threat model to the team that ships it. I'm currently Technical Lead at Primelis, and on the side I build local-first software.
